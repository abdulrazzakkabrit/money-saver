Privacy Policy

Money Saver · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Draft: this policy must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer in the places where you operate before it is published. Items in [brackets] must be completed.

1. Who we are

Money Saver is a personal budget app operated by Stelen95. Contact: the Feedback box in the app's Settings. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

2. What information we collect

  • Account details: your name, your email address and a password. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash. We cannot read it.
  • Verification: the one-time codes we send you and the time they were sent. Codes expire quickly and are deleted when used or expired.
  • Your budget data: the income, bills, expenses, savings, debts, savings goals, categories, notes, dates, and currency settings you enter. This is the core of the service.
  • Technical data: basic information needed to keep the service secure, such as sign-in times and failed sign-in counts. We do not use advertising trackers.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers, or your location. Receipt photos are read on your device to suggest a total and store, and the photo is not stored after it is read.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and to send verification codes.
  • To save your budget so you can see it from any signed-in device.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to questions and requests you send us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your budget data for advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — stores accounts and budget data.
  • Brevo — delivers verification codes sent by email.
  • ExchangeRate-API and similar rate services — receive only a request for currency rates. They receive no personal information.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account and budget data for as long as your account is active. When you request account deletion, your account is scheduled for deletion 10 days later. You can cancel the request during those 10 days. After 10 days, your account and budget data are deleted from our live systems. Copies in backups are deleted within 10 days after that.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict some uses. To exercise these rights, use the Delete account option in Settings or contact us through the Feedback box in the app's Settings. We respond as soon as we can.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, one-time codes that expire, limits on repeated sign-in attempts, and database rules that stop any user from reading another user's data. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your phone and email account protected.

8. Children

Money Saver is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children under that age. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Your information may be processed in [countries] where our providers operate. We take steps to protect it in line with this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: use the Feedback box in the app's Settings.
