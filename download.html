What it does

Everything about your month, in one place.

No spreadsheets to build. Add what comes in and what goes out, and the app does the rest.

📅

Monthly overview

Income, bills, expenses, savings and what is left, for each month. Swipe between months to compare.

📷

Receipt scanner

Take a photo of a receipt. The app reads the total and suggests a category, so you only check it.

🔔

Reminders

Set a note or a reminder for a bill or a debt, and get a notification at the time you choose.

Charts

See where every unit of money goes.

The chart shows each category as a slice of the month. One glance tells you which part of your budget is growing.

  • Slices for bills, food, transport and fun
  • Compare this month with the last one
  • Watch your savings grow over time

Find the waste

Spot the money you don't need to spend.

Set a planned amount for each category. The app shows you, in red, where you went over. Cut those, and the difference stays with you.

  • Red means you went over the plan
  • Small grey line shows the plan
  • Find the subscriptions and extras you forgot about

Your account

Phone and computer, same numbers.

Sign in with your own account. Your data stays in your account, not on one device, so you can open it on your phone or your computer and see the same month.

  • Private: other people with the app cannot see your numbers
  • Forgot your password? Get a 6-digit code by email
  • Guided tour for each new account
