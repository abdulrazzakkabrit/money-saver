What it does

Everything about your month, in one place.

No spreadsheets to build. Add what comes in and what goes out, and the app does the rest.

📅

Monthly overview

Income, bills, expenses, savings and what is left, for each month. Swipe between months to compare.

📷

Receipt scanner

Take a photo of a receipt. The app reads the total and suggests a category, so you only check it.

🔔

Reminders

Set a note or a reminder for a bill or a debt, and get a notification at the time you choose.

For businesses

A business account, made for owners.

Choose Business when you create your account. Shops, restaurants, workshops and salons see their day clearly and stop wasting money.

📈

Daily sales and profit

Enter each day's sales and costs. Profit before and after tax, and VAT to set aside, are worked out for you.

👥

Team, loans and bills

Salaries with pay days, loans with monthly payments, and bills. Record full or part payments.

📦

Stock and reorders

Products with quantity, price and paid or not. Say how long one order lasts, and the app reminds you to order again.

🗓

Payment calendar

Every payment on one calendar, with reminders before it is due. Reports by month and an Excel export for your accountant.

Opening a business?

A feasibility study, done for you.

Choose New business when you sign up. Enter what you need to buy, your monthly costs and what you expect to sell, and the app works out the rest.

  • Start-up costs with a ready checklist for your type of business
  • Total budget needed, break-even, payback and return on investment
  • A small, a good and a big version of your business, side by side
  • When you open, launch it: your team, costs and loan move over
Version 1.1.4

What's new

The latest update of Money Saver, for personal and business accounts.

  • A customizable quick panel on the business overview, a Schedule page for availability and hours, and status dots for who is at work.
  • Keep your personal and business accounts on one device and switch between them without logging in each time.
  • French: the app, the sign-in screen and this website are now in English, Arabic, Czech and French.
  • New currencies, and a "Your currency" choice in Settings for personal and business accounts.
  • Business mode: a short guided tour, part payments and extra loan repayments, VAT, and Excel export.
  • Phone and computer no longer overwrite each other: changes made on both are kept.
  • Business accounts are separate from personal ones. Running businesses get stock and team pay; new businesses get a feasibility study.
  • Each account keeps the type it was created with. For the other kind, create a second account.
